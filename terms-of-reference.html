Terms of Reference

Access Committee – FAIRdata4Research

IRAS: 349333

1Background

1.1Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust (NUH) and University Hospitals Leicester NHS Trust (UHL) generate significant volumes of data as part of routine care. There is currently no standardised mechanism through which researchers can access data held by the two organisations. Establishing such a mechanism is vital in the context of the new NHS England Secure Data Environment Programme.

1.2Following publication of the Goldacre Report, NHS England (NHSE) published the Data Saves Lives Strategy (and later updated their Data Access Policy) which outlined the strategic shift away from ‘sharing’ NHS data for use in Health-Related Research projects, to providing ‘access’ to those data via a network of interoperable Secure Data Environments (SDEs). SDEs will become the primary route for researchers to access NHS data required to support their projects. There are now multiple SDEs in England, and Trusted Research Environments across the UK.

1.3FAIRdata4Research is a research database that was submitted for review and was given favourable ethical opinion. Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust is the sponsor of the research database this also involved identifying the lawful basis for data processing and to ensure the protocol complies with all legal and research governance requirements. Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust is the legal entity that is responsible for the database.

1.4The purpose of this Access Committee is to review applications on behalf of the sponsor which is Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust and to help provide assurance that the proposed use of the data for research is in line with the protocol and that patient benefit can be clearly understood and articulated.

1.5The Access Committee is not the only governance function that is present for FAIRdata4Research. The Management Committee provides strategic oversight and assurance of the FairData4Resesarch research database (IRAS:349333), and to provide the oversight of the associated Data Access Committee (DAC). The Chairs of the Access Committee are present on the Management Committee. The Management Committee connects into the wider governance structures of the Research and Innovation Teams.

1.6The chain of governance is that the Access Committee is accountable to the Management Committee which manages and monitors the research database across all functions. The Management Committee is accountable to the Research and Innovation Quality and Assurance Committee which will receive reports from the Management Committee.

1.7Requests will be received via a form similar to that used by the SDE programme. This is to reduce the burden on researchers, so the same form can be passed on to this Access Committee without requiring any additional paperwork.

1.8Should the Access Committee have concerns regarding process, procedures or how to assess a particular application, they should be raised by the Access Committee Chair(s) to the Management Committee or via the Administrative support provided to the Access Committee.

2Functions of the Access Committee

2.1The primary function of the Access Committee is to provide a documented and transparent process for researchers to apply for and be granted access to the data that is available from FAIRdata4Research data collection centres. It will review the application from the researcher with these considerations

2.2Safe Projects

2.2.1Has a medical purpose which aligns with helping the public’s health and/or benefiting the NHS been clearly described?

2.2.2Benefits a wide enough group of people and has a significant impact on them.

2.2.3Lines up with NHS priorities.

2.2.4Generates information which can be used later by others

2.3Safe People

2.3.1Research organisations should be considered carefully – they must have values similar to those of the NHS and have data protection policies in place.

2.3.2There will be criteria which could mean that an organisation is denied access to the data, such as not having data security in place, international sanctions and/or which sector or industry an organisation is from (e.g., a business sector that goes against the values of the NHS)

2.3.3There must be a named specific person who will be accessing the data, and they must sign an agreement with the NHS about how they will use the data. They must also receive training from the NHS on how to access and use the data.

2.4Safe Settings

The Management Committee will share a list of approved Safe Settings that are allowed to be used by a researcher. Where a researcher is requesting the use of an environment not on this list, the access request should still be evaluated, and the Management Committee asked to evaluate the proposed setting.

2.5Safe Data

The Access Committee must carefully consider whether there’s any risk that an individual’s data could be de-anonymised by a research organisation, or as a result of their research. They must consider:

2.5.1What data the research organisation has asked for – and what data they already have which they might combine it with.

2.5.2Whether there is any way that the data they have requested could be used improperly.

They should try to make sure that:

2.5.3The research organisation is accessing only the data it needs for the research it’s doing – and nothing it doesn’t need.

2.5.4The research organisation has explained clearly why it needs the specific data it has asked for.

2.6Safe Outputs

The Access Committee must consider what the outputs of the research will look like and make sure that:

2.6.1There is no risk to the privacy of patients.

2.6.2Data can only be removed with permission.

2.6.3If required, there are additional conditions set on onwards publication and use.

2.7The Access Committee administration team will ensure that a data sharing agreement is in place with the organisation providing the approved Safe Setting ahead of any data being released by each Data Collection Centre. They will also ensure that the relevant agreements are in place for all organisations that have named individuals that will require access to the data.

2.8The output should be documented whether this is approval, referral for reapplication or rejection for the research to proceed with the additional details.

3Membership

3.1The Access Committee will be co-chaired by member of the NUH R&I Senior Leadership Team and a lay member, and this group is responsible for making decisions in relation to applications submitted by researchers for access to data.

3.2The Committee will be made up of representatives from the Data Controllers across the East Midlands and academics from partner organisations. Each data collection site will provide:

  • A research governance lead
  • A clinical lead
  • A research lead
  • PPIE representative
  • A technical lead

Each Committee member should have a nominated deputy that can attend on their behalf when they are conflicted on a decision or cannot comment in a timely manner due to leave.

3.3For each research project a domain expert will be brought in, therefore, if there is a project in a particular disease, they will be brought in for that specific application to ensure the context and the research is understood.

3.4Members of the Access Committee must not have any vested interest in the research proposal in any way and all members must declare any interest before conducting any review.

3.5Members of the Access Committee will be bound to abide by all NUH policies and any conflicts of interest for an application should be noted before the application is reviewed.

4Quorum

4.1A quorum will consist of the relevant full membership of the Access Committee (or nominated deputies). The relevance is dependent on the data being requested, therefore all representatives from a data collection site must be present/have commented for a decision to be made for that data collection site

5Meetings

5.1The committee will generally be convened and held as a ‘virtual’ meeting where access requests are circulated securely by e-mail (or other secure communication methods) to relevant committee members for their review in order to expedite requests.

5.2Committee members should complete their review within 2 weeks of receiving the application unless there is a need for this to be expedited in which case the timeframe will be outlined when such application is circulated for review.

5.3The committee will meet every two months to review progress and to discuss any applications which require greater detailed discussion that cannot be resolved by email communication.

5.4Applications will normally be reviewed through the routine review process. Committee meetings will be reserved for applications requiring collective discussion, where reviewer recommendations cannot be reconciled, or where significant governance concerns or matters outside the delegated authority of the Access Committee have been identified. Where appropriate, such matters will be referred to the Management Committee.

6Administration

6.1The Access Committee will be administered by administration support within the NIHR Nottingham Biomedical Research Centre.

6.2The administration and support function will receive, register and coordinate applications, undertake initial administrative checks, coordinate reviewer comments, maintain Committee records and distribute communications as necessary to support the effective operation of the Access Committee. Administrative staff will also coordinate any clarification requests to applicants and facilitate escalation of governance matters where required.

7Duties – Decision Making and Responsibilities

7.1The Access Committee is responsible for making decisions relating to applications for access to FAIRdata4Research data within its delegated authority. Matters relating to programme governance, organisational suitability, policy interpretation or other issues outside the Committee’s remit will be referred to the FAIRdata4Research Management Committee for consideration.

7.2The Access Committee shall use its best endeavours to make decisions for the approval by coming to a consensus of views. Decisions to approve or reject applications are taken by each Data Collection Centre, therefore, one data collection centre cannot compel another to provide data. Therefore, if an application only requires data from one data collection centre, they alone will make the decision. To access data via FairData4Resesarch decisions will be taken as made by consensus where possible. If there are dissenting voices the Chair will make a note of this and take a position with the option to bring the application to the next formal meeting of the Access Committee

7.3All votes of the members will remain confidential so as not to cause difficulties for any committee member outside of the meeting.

7.4The committee will either:

  • Approve the application unconditionally; or
  • Approve the application conditionally (such as data from a subset of Data Collection Sites); or
  • Reject the application with the ability to reapply; or
  • Reject the application without the ability to reapply.

7.5We will expect all approved research projects to provide details of the outcomes of the research and to capture any impacts, this will be in the form of an annual report requested from each researcher.

8Terms of office

8.1There is no maximum term of office for any Committee Member.

9Effectiveness

9.1The effectiveness of these Terms of Reference will be reviewed annually by the Access Committee. A report will be submitted to the FAIRdata4Research Management Committee for consideration. Any approved recommendations will be implemented and reported through the established Research & Innovation governance arrangements.
