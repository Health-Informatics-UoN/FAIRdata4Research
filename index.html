FAIR Data for Research

A standardised, transparent process for researchers to access NHS data for research across the East Midlands.

About FAIRdata4Research

Across healthcare providers there are significant operational challenges involved in making data available for research. The current process is time consuming, open to error and not easily repeatable. Our ambition is to develop a standardised and transparent process for researchers to access data that can be replicated at other study sites within the East Midlands.

Available Data

Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust (NUH) is the founding Data Collection Centre for FAIRdata4Research. FAIRdata4Research is open to other NHS organisations in the East Midlands to join, and the clinical specialty datasets available are continually expanding.
